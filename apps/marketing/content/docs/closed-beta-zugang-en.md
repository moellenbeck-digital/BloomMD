# Closed beta access

Web access to BloomMD (bloommd.app) has two states. The signup page shows which one applies: if you
see the form right away, registration is open. If you see a notice that your cloud access is not
enabled yet, an invitation is required.

**Only Web, cloud workspaces, sync and collaboration are ever restricted.** The
[browser demo](/demo?lang=en), [desktop builds](/download?lang=en) and
[Obsidian plugin](/obsidian?lang=en) are always available without an invitation.

## Registration is open

You need neither an invitation nor a code.

1. Open the signup page at bloommd.app.
2. Enter your email address and a password of at least 10 characters containing letters and numbers.
3. Accept the privacy policy and the terms. No account is created without your consent.
4. You receive a confirmation email. Its link is valid for 24 hours.
5. Open the link. Only then can you sign in; without a confirmed email address there is neither
   a login nor a session.
6. Sign in at bloommd.app with your email and password.

Registration is rate limited against mass signups. After too many attempts in a short time the page
shows "Too many attempts"; wait about an hour and try again.

### The confirmation email did not arrive

Check your spam and promotions folders. On the sign-in page you can resend the confirmation email.
If sending was not possible, signup shows an error instead of the "email sent" notice; you can try
again later.

### Forgot your password

Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. You receive a link that is valid for 60 minutes. After a
reset, existing sign-ins are ended.

## Beta closed (invitation only)

A waitlist entry is not an account, and a beta invitation does not create a password. The steps are
intentionally separate so that every stage is clear.

1. Join the beta waitlist at bloommd.io.
2. Confirm your email address using the confirmation email.
3. An admin or staff member invites confirmed waitlist entries.
4. You receive a personal beta invitation with an account-creation link.
5. Open the link. The invited email address is already filled in.
6. Choose your own password, accept the privacy policy and the terms, then confirm the account
   email.
7. Sign in at bloommd.app with your email and password.

### No invitation yet

Join the waitlist at bloommd.io first. In this state an account is not enabled without an
invitation or invitation code.

### You received an invitation

The invitation email links directly to account creation. Personal invitations normally do not need
a separate code. A code field may be shown as a fallback when an admin sent a code separately.

### Resending an invitation

If a waitlist confirmation or beta invitation did not arrive, an admin can resend it from waitlist
administration. Check your spam and promotions folders first.

## You already created an account

If the account exists and the email is confirmed, use the sign-in page at bloommd.app. Password
recovery is useful after an account with a password has been created. This applies in both states;
existing accounts stay unchanged.

## Desktop app

The macOS and Omarchy/Linux desktop apps work locally with your Markdown files and do not need a
BloomMD account. An account is only needed for the Web app, cloud workspaces, sync, and
collaboration.
