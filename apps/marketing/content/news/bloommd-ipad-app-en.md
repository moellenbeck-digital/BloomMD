# BloomMD for iPad and iPhone: beta, App Store release in preparation

BloomMD is now available as its own app for iPad and iPhone. It is a beta; the App Store release is
being prepared.

The app opens a folder from the Files app – for example your Obsidian vault in iCloud Drive – and
edits the Markdown files right there. Every heading is a node, and the file stays an ordinary
Markdown note. Without a folder, BloomMD works in its own folder on the device. You don't need an
account for any of this.

- **Only real changes are saved.** Opening a note never rewrites it.
- **No silent overwrites.** If another device changed the note in the meantime, the app keeps your
  version as a separate note next to it and opens the new version.
- **Handwriting with Apple Pencil.** Write formulas and sketches in the node editor. Drawings are SVG
  files in the vault – the same ones the Obsidian plugin shows and edits.
- **Map, outline and presentation.** The same note as a map, as an indented list or node by node.
- On iPad the node editor sits in a side panel next to the map, on iPhone in a sheet below it.

Signing in with a BloomMD account is optional (beta): you sign in in the browser, the app keeps the
access in the device keychain, and signing out revokes it. Your local notes need no account.

The app follows your device language (German or English) and also runs on Macs with Apple silicon.
