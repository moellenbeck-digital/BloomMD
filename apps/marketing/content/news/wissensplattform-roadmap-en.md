# Knowledge platform roadmap: ten themes, one clear starting point

BloomMD remains a locally usable visual workspace for your own Markdown files. The [internal learnings pilot](https://github.com/moellenbeck-digital/BloomMD/issues/116) is the first backend use case, testing how sources, validity, and reviewable changes work in a real team workflow.

The [public roadmap](/en/roadmap) summarizes four planning steps: the pilot and knowledge model, sources and sync, rights and cited search, then lifecycle, agent workflows, governance, and operations. The [complete planning inventory](https://github.com/moellenbeck-digital/BloomMD/blob/main/docs/WISSENSPLATTFORM-ROADMAP.md) describes all ten themes and their open decisions.

These milestones do not promise features or dates. The macOS beta and local Markdown use remain the priority.
