BloomMD shows Markdown notes as a mind map. Every heading becomes a node, and every node keeps its text. The file stays an ordinary Markdown file – readable in Obsidian, in any text editor, and ten years from now.

Your Obsidian vault, edited in place
Pick a folder with the Android folder picker – in internal storage, on an SD card or in a sync folder (for example Syncthing or Nextcloud). BloomMD edits the notes where they are: no import, no copy. Access is kept across restarts. If another device changed a note in the meantime, BloomMD does not overwrite it but keeps your version as a separate note next to it.

Handwriting with a stylus
Write formulas, arrows and sketches right inside a node. Styluses such as the S Pen or USI pens draw with pressure, fingers pan the canvas, and a resting palm does not draw. Use the lasso to move, scale and delete parts of a drawing, and link handwritten words to other notes. Every drawing is an SVG file next to your notes – in the same format as the BloomMD plugin for Obsidian.

Map, outline, presentation
Switch between the mind map, an outline for quick reading and a presentation view. Nodes can be tasks with status and due date, or decisions. On tablets the node editor opens as a side panel next to the map, on phones as a bottom sheet.

No account, works offline
BloomMD works without signing in and without an internet connection. Without a folder, your maps are Markdown files in the app's storage. Import Markdown files, and share any map as Markdown from the share menu. Opening a note never rewrites it – only real changes are saved.

Links into the app
Links like bloommd://open open a note of the current folder right at a specific node.

Optional: BloomMD account (beta)
Sign in with a BloomMD account in the browser and edit maps from your cloud workspaces together in real time. You enter your password only on the BloomMD website; the sign-in is protected by the Android Keystore, and signing out revokes it. Self-hosted BloomMD servers are supported too. Local folders need no account.

Privacy
BloomMD contains no tracking, no ads and no analytics. Your notes leave the device only when you share them or deliberately sign in with your BloomMD account.
